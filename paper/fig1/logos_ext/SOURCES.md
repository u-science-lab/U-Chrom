# External logos for Figure 1 — sources and usage terms

Collected 2026-09-24 with curl (browser User-Agent), only from official project
websites, GitHub orgs/repos or official docs. All SVGs were checked to be real
SVG documents (not HTML). `preview.png` is a labelled contact sheet (SVGs
rendered with `rsvg-convert`).

General note: using a logo *only to identify* a tool or resource the work uses
(nominative use) is normally allowed under trademark law and under the policies
quoted below. Do not recolour, crop, distort or combine logos, and do not place
them in a way that suggests endorsement. Genome Biology (open access, CC BY)
publishes figures under CC BY, but third-party marks are not relicensed by that;
a line in the figure legend such as "Logos are trademarks of their respective
owners and are used for identification only" is recommended.
