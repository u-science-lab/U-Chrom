**Supplementary Figure S1 — Fig. 2 supplement: access by format beyond one cell (a, b); groups from one modality, structure from another (c–e); linked vs copied contacts (f–i).** **a**, One trace and **b**, one chromosome across formats (Takei 2025 cerebellum whole-cell subsets, 62 per-spot tracks): the time to open (backed chromdata.zarr) or load (FOF-CT CSV, AnnData `.h5ad`) or lazily open (SpatialData) the file, then get the trace or chromosome (same protocol as Fig. 2d, which shows one cell; median of 20 random ids × 3 fresh processes). Open markers (1e8 spots): replicated cells (Fig. 2). **c**–**i**, scHiCAR mouse primary motor cortex (5,313 cells; per-cell 1 Mb `.scool`). **c**, Leiden clustering computed in ChromData on the RNA PCA (`uchrom.emb.cluster_cells`, 16 clusters; not the published labels) groups the cells; three contrasting clusters are coloured on the RNA UMAP (other clusters grey): oligodendrocytes (cluster 5; *Plp1*, *St18*, *Prr5l*, *Mobp*; n = 294), astrocytes (cluster 3; *Gpc5*, *Slc1a3*, *Atp1a2*; n = 448) and neurons (cluster 0; no glial or vascular marker expression; n = 1,336). **d**, Pseudo-bulk contact maps of the neuron and oligodendrocyte clusters, summed from the linked per-cell `.scool` through the shared index (`Dataset.contacts(group=…)`, the path the web browser uses; chr2:40–90 Mb, 1 Mb bins, each normalised to its cis contacts, shared colour scale). Pseudo-bulk from the per-cell `.scool` equals the published per-type `.cool` for 22/22 cell types (coarsened to 1 Mb; max |Δ| = 0 over 4.2 M pixels, all chromosomes). **e**, Contact probability vs distance, P(s), over all chromosomes: slopes over 2–30 Mb of −0.83 (neurons), −0.67 (oligodendrocytes) and −0.67 (astrocytes) — a modest difference. **f**, Time to sum the pseudo-bulk of chr1 vs cells aggregated, first query (solid) and cached (LRU; dashed). **g**, On-disk size. Left: the ChromData store alone when the per-cell contact maps are *linked* (the store records the `.scool` path, bin size and cell naming; 5 MB) or *copied* (every cell's pixels written into the store as a Parquet table, zstd-3; 135 MB). Right: the user's total footprint, where the community `.scool` (253 MB, hatched) is kept either way: 258 MB linked vs 388 MB copied. **h**, Import time on the `.chromdata.zarr` container, fresh process, median of 3 (whiskers min–max): linking 0.34 s vs copying all 51 M pixels 7.6 s. **i**, Whole-genome cis pseudo-bulk of each of the 22 cell types, queried through the shared index (`Dataset.contacts(group=…)`, the web browser's path), summed over all 21 chromosomes: first query (solid) vs the same query again (LRU cache; dashed); every one of these pseudo-bulks equals the published per-type `.cool` (**d**).
