**Supplementary Figure S1 — Access by format beyond one cell (a, b); groups from one modality, structure from another (c–e); linked contacts: set-up, query speed and storage (f–h).** **a**, **b** Time to open or load the file and get one trace (**a**) or one chromosome (**b**), for the data of Fig. 2c; median of 20 random ids. At 1e7 spots, one trace: chromdata.zarr 42 ms, SpatialData 0.74 s, AnnData 7.6 s. One chromosome: chromdata.zarr 0.56 s with all 62 per-spot tracks (solid) and 49 ms with coordinates only (dashed), SpatialData 0.89 s, AnnData 7.8 s. FOF-CT stops at 1e6 spots (8.4 s; memory limit). Open markers, replicated cells (Fig. 2). **c**–**h** scHiCAR mouse primary motor cortex, 5,313 cells, per-cell 1-Mb contact maps in a linked .scool file. **c** Three of 16 Leiden clusters of the RNA profiles, computed in ChromData (other clusters grey), on the RNA UMAP: oligodendrocytes (cluster 5; *Plp1*, *St18*, *Prr5l*, *Mobp*; n = 294), astrocytes (cluster 3; *Gpc5*, *Slc1a3*, *Atp1a2*; n = 448) and neurons (cluster 0; no glial or vascular markers; n = 1,336). **d** Pseudo-bulk contact maps of the neuron and oligodendrocyte clusters, summed from the linked per-cell maps through the shared index (chr2:40–90 Mb, 1-Mb bins, normalised to cis contacts). For all 22 published cell types they equal maps binned directly from the contact pairs (4.2 M pixels, maximum absolute difference 0). **e** log2(oligodendrocytes / neurons) of the maps in **d**. Away from the diagonal 75 % of pixels are higher in oligodendrocytes; the map is noisy at 1 Mb and illustrates a downstream comparison, not a biological claim. **f** Time to attach the per-cell maps to a store: link (path, bin size, cell map; 0.34 s) or copy all 51 M pixels (7.6 s); whiskers, min–max. **g** Pseudo-bulk query time, first query (filled markers, solid) and the same query again (cached; open markers, dashed). Left, whole-genome cis maps of each of the 22 cell types against the cells of the type; right, chr1 for random groups of 50 to 5,313 cells. **h** On-disk size. Left, the store alone with the maps linked (5 MB) or copied (135 MB). Right, the total footprint, which keeps the community .scool (253 MB, hatched) either way: 258 MB linked against 388 MB copied.
