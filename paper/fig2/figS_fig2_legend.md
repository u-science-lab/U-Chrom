**Supplementary Figure S1 — Fig. 2 supplement: access by format beyond one cell (a, b); groups from one modality, structure from another (c–e); linked contacts: set-up, query speed and storage (f–h).** **a**, One trace and **b**, one chromosome across formats (Takei 2025 cerebellum whole-cell subsets, 62 per-spot tracks): the time to open (backed chromdata.zarr) or load (FOF-CT CSV, AnnData `.h5ad`) or lazily open (SpatialData) the file, then get the trace or chromosome (same protocol as Fig. 2d, which shows one cell; median of 20 random ids × 3 fresh processes). At 1e7 spots, one trace: chromdata.zarr 42 ms, SpatialData 0.74 s, AnnData 7.6 s; one chromosome: chromdata.zarr 0.56 s with all 62 per-spot tracks (solid; it reads a slice of every cell-sorted row group) and 49 ms with coordinates only (dashed), SpatialData 0.89 s, AnnData 7.8 s. FOF-CT stops at 1e6 spots (8.4 s) because loading the 1e7-spot CSV exceeds our 40 GB peak-memory safety limit. Open markers (1e8 spots): replicated cells (Fig. 2). **c**–**h**, scHiCAR mouse primary motor cortex (5,313 cells; per-cell 1 Mb `.scool`). **c**, Leiden clustering computed in ChromData on the RNA PCA (`uchrom.emb.cluster_cells`, 16 clusters; not the published labels) groups the cells; three contrasting clusters are coloured on the RNA UMAP (other clusters grey): oligodendrocytes (cluster 5; *Plp1*, *St18*, *Prr5l*, *Mobp*; n = 294), astrocytes (cluster 3; *Gpc5*, *Slc1a3*, *Atp1a2*; n = 448) and neurons (cluster 0; no glial or vascular marker expression; n = 1,336). **d**, Pseudo-bulk contact maps of the neuron and oligodendrocyte clusters, summed from the linked per-cell `.scool` through the shared index (`Dataset.contacts(group=…)`, the path the web browser uses; chr2:40–90 Mb, 1 Mb bins, each normalised to its cis contacts, shared colour scale). Pseudo-bulk from the per-cell `.scool` equals the published per-type `.cool` for 22/22 cell types (coarsened to 1 Mb; max |Δ| = 0 over 4.2 M pixels, all chromosomes). **e**, Difference between the two groups in the same window: log2(oligodendrocytes / neurons) of the pseudo-bulk maps in **d**, each normalised to its cis contacts (colour bar clipped at the 98th percentile of |log2 ratio|). Away from the diagonal 75 % of pixels are higher in oligodendrocytes, i.e. their pseudo-bulk has relatively more longer-range contacts; the map is noisy at 1 Mb and serves to show a downstream comparison on the queried data, not a biological claim. **f**, Linking is fast to set up: time to attach the per-cell `.scool` to the `.chromdata.zarr` store, fresh process, median of 3 (whiskers min–max). Linking records the path, bin size and cell mapping (0.34 s); copying writes all 51 M pixels into the store (7.6 s). **g**, Linked contacts are fast to query: pseudo-bulk summed from the linked per-cell `.scool` through the shared index (`Dataset.contacts(group=…)`, the web browser's path); first query (filled markers, solid) and the same query again (LRU cache; open markers, dashed). Left: each of the 22 cell types, whole-genome cis (all 21 chromosomes) vs the cells of the type; every one of these pseudo-bulks equals the published per-type `.cool` (**d**). Right: chr1 for random groups of 50 to 5,313 cells. **h**, Linking saves storage. Left: the ChromData store alone when the per-cell contact maps are *linked* (5 MB) or *copied* (every cell's pixels written into the store as a Parquet table, zstd-3; 135 MB). Right: the user's total footprint, where the community `.scool` (253 MB, hatched) is kept either way: 258 MB linked vs 388 MB copied.
