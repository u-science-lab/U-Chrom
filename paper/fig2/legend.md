**Figure 2 — One ChromData store serves tracing data at real scale, alongside the formats the community uses.** **a**, The ChromData data model: `spots` (one row per observation) point at a shared locus axis (`bins`) through `bin_id`, and at `traces` and `cells` through `trace_id` and `cell_id`; coordinates (+ layers) and per-spot tracks are row-aligned with the spots, contact maps are linked by bins and a cell map rather than copied. Right: the `x.chromdata.zarr` layout (format 2.2) as written by `uchrom.core.zarrcd`; the glyph at each node gives its storage type (Parquet table, Zarr array, JSON attributes). Every spot-aligned value is stored once: coordinates and keys in one Parquet table per chromosome, every other spot-aligned column in cell-sorted primary tables, and spot columns that only repeat a value of their bin, cell or trace once per key; `index/` maps the (cell, trace, chromosome) runs of the two sides one to one. **b**, Interoperability with community formats: what survives an export followed by an import (`benchmarks/fig2/f_roundtrip.py`; values compared exactly, coordinates to the format's precision): filled, preserved; half-filled, partial, or not representable in that format; open, not applicable. PDB is export-only and .3dg import-only in U-Chrom. **c**, File size and full-read time (into memory) vs number of spots for whole-cell subsets of the Takei et al. 2025 cerebellum DNA seqFISH+ data (62 per-spot tracks), for chromdata.zarr 2.2 and the storage schemes publicly used by the community for chromatin-tracing data — the 4DN FOF-CT core table (CSV, official `uchrom.io.write_fofct` / `ChromData.from_fofct`), AnnData with spots as observations (`write_h5ad` / `read_h5ad`) and SpatialData (spots as a 3-D points element, cells as a table; `spatialdata` 0.7.2 defaults, full read = `read_zarr` + `.compute()`); median of 3 fresh processes. At 1e7 spots chromdata.zarr stores 1.8 GB and reads in 0.57 s (FOF-CT 6.7 GB, 99 s; AnnData 5.6 GB, 5.4 s; SpatialData 2.2 GB, 2.2 s). Flat Parquet, the storage engine under chromdata.zarr and therefore its lower bound without hierarchy, index or metadata, and the ChromData format versions are in Supplementary Fig. S1. **d**, Time per query vs spots: backed chromdata.zarr from disk (`ChromData.read(path, backed=True)`; `get_trace`, `get_cell`, `get_chrom` with all 62 tracks and with coordinates only, and opening the store; median of 20 random ids × 3 fresh processes, shading IQR) vs loading the whole table in memory, then subsetting (FOF-CT, AnnData; load + one `get_trace`) and SpatialData's idiomatic lazy route (open + a dask filter on `trace_id` + `.compute()`). At 1e7 spots `get_trace` takes 6.2 ms and `get_cell` 9.0 ms; `get_chrom` with all tracks reads a slice of every cell-sorted row group (0.52 s; 13 ms with coordinates only). **e**, Beyond RAM. Left: memory used to open the data and answer the trace and cell queries of **d** (above the interpreter's baseline), backed chromdata.zarr vs the whole table loaded (colours as in **d**). Right: exact median 3-D distance map of chr11 (`uchrom.fea.distance_map`), peak memory vs traces when the store is loaded whole and when `iter_traces` streams it under a 2 GB budget (1.55 vs 12.3 GB at 1e7 spots); streamed and in-memory medians are bitwise identical at every size (inset: 1e7 spots, 10.7 M bin pairs; at 1e8 against the chromosome loaded alone). Open markers (1e8 spots in **d** and **e**): Takei rep 1 replicated ten times with renamed cells and traces (`benchmarks/fig2/replicate_store.py`), not new cells; at 1e8 loading the whole table does not fit in 64 GB of RAM. **f**, Groups from one modality, structure from another (scHiCAR mouse primary motor cortex, 5,313 cells). Leiden clustering computed in ChromData on the RNA PCA (`uchrom.emb.cluster_cells`, 16 clusters; not the published labels) groups the cells; three contrasting clusters are shown on the RNA UMAP: oligodendrocytes (Plp1, St18, Prr5l, Mobp; n = 294), astrocytes (Gpc5, Slc1a3, Atp1a2; n = 448) and neurons (no glial or vascular marker expression; n = 1,336). Their pseudo-bulk contact maps are summed from the linked per-cell `.scool` through the shared index (`Dataset.contacts(group=…)`, the path the web browser uses; chr2:40–90 Mb, 1 Mb bins, each normalised to its cis contacts). Contact probability vs distance, P(s), over all chromosomes: slopes over 2–30 Mb of −0.83 (neurons), −0.67 (oligodendrocytes) and −0.67 (astrocytes) — a modest difference. Pseudo-bulk from the per-cell `.scool` equals the published per-type `.cool` for 22/22 cell types (max |Δ| = 0 over 4.2 M pixels, all chromosomes). Right: time to sum the pseudo-bulk of chr1 vs cells aggregated, first query and cached. Machine: Apple M5 Pro (18 cores), 64 GB RAM, macOS 26.4; warm OS page cache (files not flushed between write and read). Data and scripts: `paper/fig2/results/*.json`, `benchmarks/fig2/`.
