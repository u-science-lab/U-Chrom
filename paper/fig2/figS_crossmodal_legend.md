**Supplementary Figure S2 — Groups from one modality, structure from another (scHiCAR mouse primary motor cortex, 5,313 cells).** **a**, Leiden clustering computed in ChromData on the RNA PCA (`uchrom.emb.cluster_cells`, 16 clusters; not the published labels) groups the cells; three contrasting clusters are coloured on the RNA UMAP: oligodendrocytes (cluster 5; *Plp1*, *St18*, *Prr5l*, *Mobp*; n = 294), astrocytes (cluster 3; *Gpc5*, *Slc1a3*, *Atp1a2*; n = 448) and neurons (cluster 0; no glial or vascular marker expression; n = 1,336). **b**, Pseudo-bulk contact maps of the neuron and oligodendrocyte clusters, summed from the linked per-cell `.scool` through the shared index (`Dataset.contacts(group=…)`, the path the web browser uses; chr2:40–90 Mb, 1 Mb bins, each normalised to its cis contacts). Pseudo-bulk from the per-cell `.scool` equals the published per-type `.cool` for 22/22 cell types (max |Δ| = 0 over 4.2 M pixels, all chromosomes). **c**, Contact probability vs distance, P(s), over all chromosomes: slopes over 2–30 Mb of −0.83 (neurons), −0.67 (oligodendrocytes) and −0.67 (astrocytes) — a modest difference. **d**, Time to sum the pseudo-bulk of chr1 vs cells aggregated, first query and cached (LRU). Data: `paper/fig2/results/f_crossmodal.json` (`benchmarks/fig2/f_crossmodal.py`), `e_query.json`, `e_contacts.json`; machine: Apple M5 Pro, 64 GB, macOS 26.4, warm OS cache.
